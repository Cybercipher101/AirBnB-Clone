About this space

Perched high upon the dramatic cliffs of Malibu, The Glass House is a striking modernist marvel designed to blur the boundary between indoor luxury and the wild beauty of the Pacific Ocean. Featuring soaring 14-foot glass walls, imported Italian terrazzo flooring, and custom walnut cabinetry, every corner has been engineered for effortless tranquility.

Show more >

Step out onto the expansive cantilevered teak deck where an infinity pool mirrors the ocean horizon. In the evening, gather around the built-in gas fire pit while listening to the crashing surf below.

The chef's kitchen boasts Miele appliances, a Sub-Zero wine preservation column, and an expansive Calacatta marble island. The primary master wing features an open-concept soaking tub overlooking Point Dume, an infrared sauna, and automated blackout shades for restorative sleep.
