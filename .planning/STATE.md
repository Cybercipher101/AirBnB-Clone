## Architectural Invariants & Decisions Upheld
1. **Zero JavaScript:** 100% of interactions (Save toggle, photo lightbox modal, amenities modal, guest dropdown, description fold) driven by pure HTML5 and CSS3 (`:target`, `<details>`, `:checked`, `:hover`).
2. **Pure Vanilla CSS:** No external frameworks, preprocessors, or bundlers.
3. **MNC-Grade Design Fidelity:** Adheres to 8pt modular grid, Gestalt grouping, WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, and Airbnb design language.
